# Code of conduct

This project wants to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or the
language they speak. By taking part (issues, pull requests, discussions), you agree to follow these rules.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in their second language.
- Give constructive, specific feedback on the work, never on the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Accept that the maintainer has the final say on what goes into the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, harassment, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information (address, email, keys...).
- Trolling, spamming, or repeatedly pushing a topic after a decision has been made.
- Sharing pirated software, cracks or licence keys in issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by contacting the maintainer, [@Robocnop](https://github.com/Robocnop), through GitHub. Reports
are handled privately. Depending on the case, the maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues, or
block someone from the project.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
